1981 C3 Corvette

A forever car. The goal is a street monster with a toned-down IMSA look: no stickers, no numbers. First it has to be safe to drive.

Where it stands

The new engine is in, the custom wiring harness is done, and the car has fired, but it hasn't been driven yet. This winter's job is steering, suspension, and brakes so it can go on the road in spring 2027.

Current setup

EngineBluePrint 396 small-block stroker, carbureted, base dressed (BP3961CTC)
Dyno496 hp and 519 lb-ft on BluePrint's engine dyno, June 2024
InductionHolley 850 double pumper on a 1 in. phenolic 4-hole spacer
FuelBluePrint 130 gph electric pump
ExhaustHooker Super Competition headers with side pipes

ElectricalCustom wiring harness, battery behind the driver's seat, electric headlight motors
DeletesA/C, radio, factory heater box

The plan

  1. Back on the road, safely

    Borgeson steering box, poly bushings, body mounts, springs and shocks, front bearings, full brake refresh, rebuilt trailing arms and 5/16 in. half shafts. In progress this winter.

  2. Body, paint, and interior

    Bodywork and fresh paint in the current color, alignment, a new single harness, new lights, and a full interior: roll bar, bucket seats, custom dash with analog gauges, carpet, sound deadening, door cards.

  3. Manual swap and driveline

    M22 four-speed, new driveshaft and half shafts, a built Dana 44 with 3.73 gears.

  4. Suspension, brakes, wheels, and widebody

    Further suspension and brake upgrades, 18 in. wheels with 335 rear and 275 front, and a widebody with 4 in. rear and 2 in. front flares.

  5. Engine

    New cam, single-plane intake, built carb, and eventually E85.
