About me

I'm Aidan. I live in Richmond, Virginia, and I like figuring out how things work, then making them work better.

At my day job that means software, data, and projects. At home it usually means a car with its wheels off. The two have more in common than you'd think: both reward patience, good notes, and not skipping steps.

How I got here

WhenWhat happened
First planI wanted to be a doctor, and got certified as an EMT as a first step toward medicine.
2019Started at Christopher Newport University, where the part of me that liked figuring out how things work won out: I studied computer engineering.
2020 to 2022Said yes to a lot on campus. I was a Resident Assistant, a Student Ambassador, Vice President of Student Government, Vice President of my fraternity, and President of CERO, the campus emergency response organization. Leading groups of people taught me more than any class did.
Summer 2022Took a summer IT job at the Virginia State Bar, helping with a workstation refresh and the help desk.
Oct 2022 to nowThat summer job turned into my career. I've spent the years since building and running the systems the Bar depends on. The details are on my resume.

What I care about

Leaving things better documented than I found them. The best system in the world is a liability if only one person understands it. I wrote my organization's first iMIS documentation for that reason.

Building for the people who use it. Most of my projects start with sitting next to someone and watching how they actually do their job, before deciding what to build.

Doing it myself when I can. Whether it's a help desk system or a steering box, I'd rather understand it well enough to build or fix it than pay someone to.

Outside of work

Cars

I do my own car work, from wiring to suspension. Right now that's a 1981 Corvette I'm bringing back to life, with a build log here on the site. My daily driver is a '95 GMC K1500.

EMT

I'm a certified Emergency Medical Technician. I originally wanted to be a doctor, and EMT felt like the right first step. At Christopher Newport I was president of CERO, the Captains Emergency Response Organization, which worked on emergency preparedness across campus: getting students CPR certified and making sure everyone had an emergency resource when they needed one.

Richmond

I live in Richmond, and it's home. It's a great city for someone who likes old cars, good food, and having a garage to work in.
