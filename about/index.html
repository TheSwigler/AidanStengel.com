About me

I'm Aidan. I grew up in the Richmond area, and I've always been the person who needs to know how things work, and then how to make them work better.

These days that means software, data, and projects at work, and a car with its wheels off at home. The two have more in common than you'd think: both reward patience, good notes, and not skipping steps.

How I work

I like every part of the job: figuring out why something's broken, building the fix, helping the people who depend on it, and running the project that ties it all together. My favorite question is "how should this work?" Give me a messy process and I'll want to map it, simplify it, and build the system it should have been all along.

I do my best work when ownership is clear and everyone knows who's doing what. As I grow into leading projects and teams, the two things I care about most are communicating clearly, so everyone understands the plan and why, and clearing blockers out of people's way so they can do their best work.

The most fun I've had so far was building our IT service desk. I've built it three times now, each version better than the last. You can read about it here.

What I care about

Leaving things better documented than I found them. The best system in the world is a liability if only one person understands it. I wrote my organization's first iMIS documentation for that reason.

Building for the people who use it. Most of my projects start with sitting next to someone and watching how they actually do their job, before deciding what to build.

Doing it myself when I can. Whether it's a help desk system or a steering box, I'd rather understand it well enough to build or fix it than pay someone to.
